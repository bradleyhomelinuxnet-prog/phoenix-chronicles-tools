ERE WE WERE · THE LOST SECRETS OF GIZA · II — THE LONG COUNT — 138.000 s · station sheet v1
Source: Jason Breshears (Archaix), "The Lost Secrets of Giza" overview. Same template, same rules as Giza I.
Shape: 3 s title + 9 × 15 s = 4,140 frames at 30 fps. The span itself is the palindrome: station 1 = 5239 BC, station 9 = 2106 AD, station 5 = 1567 BC, the exact centre (3,672 years each side).

==============================================================================
H01 · STATION 1/9 · mirror 9 · 5239 BC · Before Year One
TITLE   THE LONG COUNT
CLAIM   Breshears starts his clock 1,344 years before Year One and runs it to the capstone
FACT    From 5239 BC to 2106 AD is 7,344 years, with no year zero. Divide by three: 2,448.
NATORI  Fifty-two thirty-nine BC. Before Year One, before the pyramid, somebody was already counting.
ROGUE   Seven thousand three hundred forty-four years from here to the capstone. Hold on to that number.
CHECK   5239 BC is the start of Breshears’ Anunnaki reckoning (Chronicon) · 5239 + 2106 − 1 = 7,344, because there is no year zero.

H02 · STATION 2/9 · mirror 8 · 3440 BC · Entrance · north face
TITLE   THE DRAGON STAR
CLAIM   The descending passage points north, at the pole star of its age: Thuban, the eye of the Dragon
FACT    Thuban, Alpha Draconis, was the pole star around 2800 BC. The descending passage slopes at about 26°.
NATORI  Thirty-four forty BC. The passage in the north face points at the eye of the Dragon.
ROGUE   The old pole star. When it sat in that shaft, he says, a third of the world died.
CHECK   Precession: Thuban was nearest the pole c. 2830 BC · the 3440 BC aim and the 3439 BC catastrophe are Breshears’ readings; Herschel and Piazzi Smith gave other dates.

H03 · STATION 3/9 · mirror 7 · 1899 BC · Giza to Thebes
TITLE   TWO EGYPTS
CLAIM   Every pyramid in Egypt stands in the north; the kings of the Valley lie far to the south, with none
FACT    Giza to the Valley of the Kings is about 500 km as the crow flies. No pyramid stands there.
NATORI  Eighteen ninety-nine BC. He says the sea let go of the north, and the land rose.
ROGUE   Two Egypts. The pyramids in one, the kings’ tombs in the other, weeks of walking apart.
CHECK   Straight-line distance ≈ 500 km (311 mi); the talk’s 416 miles is nearer a road route · the 1899 BC uplift is Breshears’ Chronicon.
CLIP    pyramid on the desert horizon (df1e454e, darkened)

H04 · STATION 4/9 · mirror 6 · 1447 BC · Top of the Grand Gallery
TITLE   EXODUS 2448
CLAIM   Breshears’ number for a people leaving a dying world is 2,448, and the pyramid keeps returning it
FACT    2448 AM is 1447 BC on his calendar. 7,344 ÷ 3 = 2,448, and 204 years is 2,448 months.
NATORI  Fourteen forty-seven BC. Good people leave a bad world. He files it under one number.
ROGUE   Twenty-four forty-eight. A third of the long count. Two hundred four years, counted in months.
CHECK   3895 − 2448 = 1447 · 1447 BC is the traditional early Exodus date (from 1 Kings 6:1); many scholars prefer the 13th century BC.
CLIP    top of the Grand Gallery (49c8832b)

H05 · STATION 5/9 · mirror 5 · AXIS · 1567 BC · The King’s Chamber shaft
TITLE   THE MIDDLE YEAR
CLAIM   Fold the 7,344 years in half and Breshears lands on Megiddo, the first Armageddon
FACT    5239 BC plus 3,672 years is 1567 BC. Another 3,672 reaches 2106 AD. The exact centre.
NATORI  Fifteen sixty-seven BC. Fold the long count in half, and it creases here.
ROGUE   Megiddo, he says. The first Armageddon, sitting dead centre of the whole run.
CHECK   The arithmetic holds (no year zero) · Thutmose III’s Battle of Megiddo is usually dated 1457 or 1479 BC, not 1567.
CLIP    King's Chamber shaft looking up (92006f26)

H06 · STATION 6/9 · mirror 4 · 1303 AD · The casing quarry
TITLE   THE STRIPPING
CLAIM   Earthquakes cracked the white armour; the builders of Cairo took the rest
FACT    After the earthquake of 1303, casing stone was carted off to build Cairo. Sultan Hassan’s mosque is said to hold some.
NATORI  Thirteen oh three. The ground shook, the white skin cracked, and Cairo came for the pieces.
ROGUE   Sixty years of hauling. What the flood could not strip, a building boom did.
CHECK   Al-Maqrizi records the 1303 quake · the Sultan Hassan attribution is a long tradition, not a surveyed fact.
CLIP    casing blocks half-buried (b325083c)

H07 · STATION 7/9 · mirror 3 · 1765 AD · Above the King’s Chamber
TITLE   DAVISON’S CHAMBER
CLAIM   Five low rooms stacked over the King’s Chamber carry the mountain’s weight away from it
FACT    Nathaniel Davison found the first relieving chamber in 1765. Vyse blasted into four more in 1837.
NATORI  Seventeen sixty-five. A ladder, a candle, and a crawlway into a room nobody had seen.
ROGUE   Granite beams, stacked five deep, so the chamber below never feels the weight.
CHECK   Vyse’s 1837 chambers carry the builders’ red-paint marks, including Khufu’s name, Egyptology’s key dating evidence; Breshears dates the build to 2905–2815 BC.

H08 · STATION 8/9 · mirror 2 · 1926 AD · The Sphinx enclosure
TITLE   THE GUARDIAN
CLAIM   Breshears: the body is a dog’s, Anubis at the gate, and the head was recut small
FACT    The Sphinx is about 73 metres long and 20 high. Baraize cleared its enclosure from 1925 to 1936.
NATORI  Nineteen twenty-six. They dig the sand from its paws. The head looks too small for the body.
ROGUE   He says it was a jackal once. The riddler at the gate, keeping us out of Eden.
CHECK   Egyptology: carved for Khafre, c. 2500 BC · the Anubis reading is Breshears’ (after Robert Temple).

H09 · STATION 9/9 · mirror 1 · 2106 AD · The flat summit
TITLE   THE CAPSTONE
CLAIM   Two hundred three courses stand. The last, the chief cornerstone, is missing, and he reads the gap as time
FACT    6000 AM is 2106 AD. 1,902 + 204 = 2,106, and a 612-foot face is exactly 7,344 inches.
NATORI  Twenty-one oh six. Two hundred three courses stand. The last one is still missing.
ROGUE   Set the capstone and the count closes. Six thousand years. Every stone, a name.
CHECK   6000 − 3894 = 2106 by the Chronicon rule · surveys give 201–210 courses (203 is the usual figure) and a face slope near 186 m, ≈ 611 ft.

WHERE THE TALK AND THE RECORD DIFFER — flagged on screen in the CHECK lines:
• Battle of Megiddo: usually dated 1457 or 1479 BC. The talk puts the centre of the span on it; the arithmetic centre is 1567 BC.
• Giza to the Valley of the Kings: about 500 km (311 mi) in a straight line; the talk's 416 miles is nearer a road route.
• Thuban was nearest the pole around 2830 BC. The 3440 BC aim is Breshears' reading; Herschel and Piazzi Smith gave other dates.
• Vyse's 1837 relieving chambers carry the builders' red-paint marks, including Khufu's name, which Egyptology treats as the key dating evidence.
• The face slope measures about 186 m (≈ 611 ft); the talk uses 612 ft = 7,344 in.
• Sultan Hassan mosque holding casing stone is a tradition, not a survey.
• Arithmetic checked: 5239 + 2106 − 1 = 7,344 (no year zero) · 7,344 ÷ 3 = 2,448 · 204 × 12 = 2,448 · 3895 − 2448 = 1447 · −5239 + 3,672 = 1567 BC · 1567 BC + 3,672 = 2106 AD · 612 × 12 = 7,344 · 6000 − 3894 = 2106 · 1902 + 204 = 2106.
