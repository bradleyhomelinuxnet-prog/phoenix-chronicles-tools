METHOD (repeatable on any clip in the archive):
 1. faster-whisper base.en, vad_filter on -> real segment start/end per clip.
 2. Speaker ID by pitch: per-segment median F0 via autocorrelation over 70-300 Hz.
    NATORI >= 135 Hz, ROGUE < 135 Hz.
