WHAT THIS REEL IS
  Movement I of the Master Walk ends at AM 6000 / 2106 AD -- THE CAPSTONE.
  The base-138 spine does not end there. Its 44th node is AM 6072 / 2178 AD,
  seventy-two years further on. Reel D is the walk past the end of the film
  we already made. Movement III.

RUNTIME LOCK
  23 slots x 15.000 s = 345.000 s = 138 x 2.5
  Same shape as Reel C and Nobody's Body. 1920x1080, 24 fps, 8,280 frames.

CARD ORDER
  TITLE, MIRROR, beats 01-09, AXIS, beats 10-18, FORK, LOOP.
  18 beats is even, so the palindromic axis falls BETWEEN 09 and 10 --
  the AXIS card sits in the seam, not on a beat. Beat 09 ends
  "That's the next one." Beat 10 opens "Twenty-one seventy-eight."

AUDIO
  Every source clip carries its own baked lip-synced dialogue from the
  Master Walk shoot. All of it is MUTED. Reel D's dialogue is burned
  caption only. This follows Nobody's Body, which reused these same clips
  under new narration.

LIP-SYNC RULE -- READ THIS BEFORE CUTTING
  Reused clips have mouths moving to the wrong words. Cut to segments where
  faces are turned, in profile, backlit, at distance, or backs-to-camera.
  Avoid any front-on close two-shot. Where a clip's middle third is a close
  frontal, use its outer thirds and re-time the caption.

WHY THESE CLIPS

  B01 is the epoch stone. 1135 BC is AM 2760 = 138 x 20. The walk's own
      Phoenix 20 clip IS the beat -- the stake was never arbitrary.
  B16 lands on AM 3036, the Phoenix 22 clip. The number 3036 appears twice
      in this universe in different roles: as an AM year (859 BC) and as the
      span 1135 BC -> 1902 AD. B16 is where the reel points at that.
  B21 returns to AM 1 under an unanswered question, so the LOOP card closes
      the same way Movement II closes the Master Walk.

DIALOGUE -- 18 BEATS

B01 NATORI  The old charts stopped at twenty. Nobody ran it past that.
            Twenty-two turns of one-thirty-eight is three thousand thirty-six.
            Lay that against the eleven-thirty-five stake and it doesn't drift.
            It lands. Nineteen-oh-two.
B02 ROGUE   Lands on what.
    NATORI  On the year the sky opened. And three thousand thirty-six is the
            Descendant Passage, in pyramid-inches. The corridor and the cycle
            are the same number.
B03 ROGUE   Whose inches? Every surveyor down there hands you a different figure.
    NATORI  Which is why I'm not leaning on it. Set the stone aside.
            The spine holds without it.
B04 NATORI  Twenty-one. Twenty-two. Twenty-three. Seventeen sixty-four.
            Nineteen-oh-two. Twenty forty. Three sightings, consecutive.
            They sit on it like vertebrae.
B05 ROGUE   They're a hundred thirty-eight apart. That's one fact wearing
            three coats. Unless --
    NATORI  Unless the outer two were logged before anyone drew the spine.
    ROGUE   Were they.
    NATORI  Seventeen sixty-four was.
B06 NATORI  Holo-date runs flip on eight-thirty-one. Turn it over.
            One, three, eight. The engine doesn't reference the sigil, Rogue.
            It manufactures it.
B07 NATORI  Eleven thirty-five to nineteen-oh-two. One million, one hundred
            eight thousand, eight hundred seventy-five days. Divide by the
            tropical year. Three thousand thirty-six point zero zero.
    ROGUE   That's not a witness. That's a conversion.
B08 NATORI  It would be -- if I'd chosen the days. I didn't. Both ends are
            pinned to eclipses. The count came off the sky, not off my
            arithmetic. Run it yourself.
B09 ROGUE   ...What's twenty-four.
    NATORI  Twenty-one seventy-eight. Multiply it by four and it reads itself
            backward. Eighty-seven twelve.
    ROGUE   That's cute.
    NATORI  That's the next one.

                            -- AXIS --

B10 ROGUE   Twenty-one seventy-eight.
    NATORI  Two turns past nineteen-oh-two. Not the next sighting -- the one
            after. If the spine's real, that's the one nobody alive gets to check.
B11 ROGUE   Convenient.
    NATORI  It's the opposite of convenient. It's the only claim on the board
            that can fail. Everything else is back-dated. That one's exposed.
B12 NATORI  Now do the number. Ten eighty-nine. Times nine -- ninety-eight
            oh-one. That's ten eighty-nine backward. Multiply it and it turns around.
B13 NATORI  Double it. Twenty-one seventy-eight. Times four -- eighty-seven twelve.
            Backward again. Those two are the only four-digit numbers on earth
            that do it. There is no third.
B14 ROGUE   That's decimal. That's an accident of having ten fingers.
            Write it in base twelve and your miracle evaporates.
    NATORI  Yes.
    ROGUE   ...Yes?
B15 NATORI  I'm not arguing it's cosmic. I'm arguing it's forced. Ten eighty-nine
            has nine multiples before it runs out of digits. They fold in pairs.
            Dead center, fifty-four forty-five. Reads the same either way.
B16 NATORI  Nine rungs. One palindrome at the axis. And I asked which of the nine
            lands on the one-thirty-eight spine.
    ROGUE   How many.
    NATORI  One. Twenty-one seventy-eight. I didn't pick it. The modulus did.
B17 ROGUE   You're telling me the ladder and the spine touch exactly once.
    NATORI  Once in nine. And it's the rung that turns around when you multiply it.
B18 ROGUE   And what's supposed to happen in twenty-one seventy-eight.
    NATORI  (does not answer -- keeps walking)

OPEN ITEMS

1. THE CAPSTONE FORK -- unresolved, and it is load-bearing.
   Extended Phoenix Timeline: Capstone at 2106 AD = AM 6000. Not a 138 node
   (6000 / 138 = 43.478). The spine's 44th node is AM 6072 = 2178 AD.
   Two terminal structures, 72 years apart. Three ways to rule:
     a) Capstone canon  -> spine terminates early; 2040 is the last true node;
                           beat B11 is wrong and needs rewriting.
     b) Spine canon     -> the Capstone is a round-number artifact.
     c) Both in tension -> the 72-year gap is what the reel is about.
   Reel D as written assumes (b). The FORK card at slot 22 is where a ruling
   of (c) would go instead. One line to change now.

2. DESCENDANT PASSAGE = 3036 PYRAMID-INCHES -- still unsourced.
   Whole-corridor figures don't fit (~2,300 or ~4,140 in depending on survey).
   Petrie's Point of Intersection at 1,110.64 in subtracted from the sloping
   length to the Subterranean Chamber lands near 3,030 -- so the figure is
   probably the segment BELOW the Point of Intersection, not the passage.
   B03 quarantines it on purpose. Find the source before promoting it.

3. TWELVE SPINE CLIPS speak the appendix year one year early. Reel D uses
   B04 (1763 for 1764) and B18 (1349 for 1350) from that set. The Reel D
   chyron above follows the spoken line, same as the Master Walk.
